# Fork reconstruction audit

Last updated: 2026-09-04

This ledger tracks the completed reconstruction of the FrameWorks fork on the current upstream
base. The reviewed reconstruction has been promoted to `master`. Every retained local change has
a clear origin, focused regression coverage, an ablation that fails for the intended reason, and
a clean topical replacement commit.

## Status rules

- `pending`: not yet reviewed under the strict audit standard.
- `partial`: at least one behavior is proved or rejected, but other hunks remain.
- `media-proved`: passed a focused end-to-end media matrix, but still needs a permanent automated
  regression before final reconstruction.
- `proved`: every behavior currently attributed to the original commit has focused coverage and an
  ablation result. This still does not mean the final history has been reconstructed.
- `dropped`: behavior was stale, accidental, redundant, or could not justify itself.
- `replaced`: implementation and tests have been rebuilt into final clean topical commits.
